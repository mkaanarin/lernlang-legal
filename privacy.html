Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary: LernLang has no accounts and no servers that store your data — your learning progress stays on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which uses your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking.

1. Our Approach to Privacy

LernLang is designed with a privacy-first approach. We believe your learning data belongs to you and should stay on your device. We do not operate any servers that store user data, and we do not require account creation or login.

2. Data That Stays on Your Device

The following data is created and stored exclusively on your device:

  • Learning progress: Completed lessons, quiz scores, and statistics
  • App preferences: Selected language, difficulty level, and settings
  • Exercise history: Your answers and performance data

This data is never sent to us or any third party. If you delete the app, this data is permanently removed.

Microphone and AI feedback: The microphone is used only while you do a speaking exercise. Your speech is turned into text by your device's speech recognition (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) — on the device whenever possible; we never record or store audio. Feedback in conversation practice is created by the AI model that runs on your device (Apple Intelligence or Gemini Nano); your answers are not sent to us.

3. Advertisements

LernLang displays ads through Google AdMob. The ad network may collect certain device information (such as device type and advertising identifier) to show relevant ads. You can limit ad tracking through your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Advertising on iOS, or Settings > Google > Ads on Android).

On iPhone, personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking in the system prompt; otherwise ads are not personalized.

4. Third-Party Services

LernLang uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For ad display

Each of these services has their own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

5. Children

LernLang is a language learning app suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal information gathered from any user, including children. No account, email, name, or age is required to use the app.

In Kids Mode no ads are shown, and leaving Kids Mode requires a parental check.

6. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device and we do not collect or process any personal data on our end, there is no data for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. You have full control over your data:

  • You can reset your progress from within the app settings
  • You can delete all data by uninstalling the app

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Significant changes will be communicated through the app.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: support@lernlang.app
